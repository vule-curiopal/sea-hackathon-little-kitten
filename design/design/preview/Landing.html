What parents ask us

Does my child have to hold the button?+

No. They tap it once and talk naturally. Buddy notices when they stop and turns the microphone off. If they want to finish early, one more tap does it.

Does Buddy listen all day?+

No. The microphone opens only after your child taps the button and closes when the turn ends. There is no wake word and no background listening.

Can siblings share?+

Yes. Each child can have their own Buddy. You choose which Buddy belongs to which child.

Which languages does Buddy speak?+

Vietnamese and English. Buddy answers in the language your child just used.

Can we give Buddy to someone else?+

Yes. Remove Buddy from your family in the app, and the code on the card works again for the new family.
